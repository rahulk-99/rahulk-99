## About Me

<a href="https://rahulk-99.github.io/">

Hi! I am **Rahul Kumar**, a Master's student in **Robotics Engineering** at the **University of Maryland, College Park** (graduating May 2026).

I specialize in building autonomous systems that integrate **Robot Learning**, **Perception**, **Physical AI**, and **Motion Planning**. My goal is to bridge the gap between simulation and reality, deploying robust intelligence on physical hardware.

*   💼 **Experience**: 
    *   **Robotics Software Intern** at [CapSen Robotics](https://www.capsenrobotics.com/): Established a **Sim-to-Real Robot Learning** framework for an industrial manipulator using Isaac Sim & Lab. Implemented vision **Foundation Models** for 3D perception & pose estimation. [[Demo Link](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rahul-kumar4_robotics-foundationmodel-reinforcementlearning-activity-7374143497047748608-q0gj?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAACaQtzwBHL_RIweLRH6PbPRpQbEa4fW2-oE)]
        *   **Co-op Extension**: Developed ROS-based Multi-modal perception pipelines for autonomous inspection & MLOps for large-scale datasets.

    *   **Research Assistant** at **GAMMA Lab (UMD)**: Under **[Prof. Dinesh Manocha](https://www.cs.umd.edu/people/dmanocha)**, architecting an end-to-end autonomous navigation stack that integrates semantic segmentation and sensor fusion into high-fidelity 3D world models to drive geometry-aware **VLA policies** trained on internet-scale data. [[GitHub](https://github.com/rahulk-99/Autonomous-navigation-stack)]

    *   **Product Development Engineer** at **Bharat Electronics Limited** (3.5 years): Led end-to-end development of a **object detection device** and a **Sonar system**, leveraging **GANs** to solve data scarcity and boost Machine Learning vision model performance in challenging underwater conditions in real-time.
*   🎓 **Education**:
    *   **Masters in Robotics** at **University of Maryland, College Park**
    *   **Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering** at **IIT Dharwad - India**
